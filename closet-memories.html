04 — Mapping · 2025

A Closet of Memories

Three generations of women, mapped through a place, a picture, an object, a word and a food.

Medium
Textile stitching, print, graphite
Scale
A0 glossy paper
Length
504 × 9 cm
Year
2025

I mapped childhood memories across three generations of women in my family. Places, photographs, objects, words and food helped me trace the memories of my grandmother Gulo, my mother and me.

We entered my grandmother’s wardrobe. Her name is Valjia. The wardrobe is fifty years old.

The complete map, joining family photographs, drawings, food and handwritten words in one continuous path.

An endless loop

I spoke with my mother, both grandmothers and my little sister and began collecting memories of the people, houses and places that connect us. I brought those stories together with my own memories, cherished objects and places I still visit. The map follows paths I have walked, those travelled by generations before me and those that will continue after me.

Places and family memories

Family portraits first, followed by the homes, gardens and outdoor places held in the archive.

What is kept

“I collect all the buttons I get and store them in this bag, in the wardrobe. It is good to have this many.” Keeping small things like these buttons helps me hold on to the memories attached to them.

A cycle through generations

The spiral gives a visual form to cyclical time and the looping paths of memory. It became a guide for bringing separate recollections into one continuous map.

Gathering the archive

I first laid the collected photographs, drawings and food fragments together. Making and arranging the pieces by hand let the map take shape through both material and memory.

Sorting the fragments

I moved the collected pieces into different arrangements, looking for connections between names, objects and places before fixing them into the final map.

Tracing relationships

The matrix shows which names, objects and places are connected.

Threads of feeling

A colour-coded legend follows the paths of my grandmother Gulo, my mother and me. Red marks memories that have changed over time.

A shared world in which past and present, personal and collective memories and different generations coexist peacefully—each following the path of those who came before, while leaving traces for those yet to come.

Resolution

The map brings different times and memories into a journey that cannot be followed all at once. I used the collected fragments to show how complicated our experience of space and time can be. I hope the map holds past and present, personal and shared memories and the stories of different generations—each following those who came before and leaving traces for those still to come.
