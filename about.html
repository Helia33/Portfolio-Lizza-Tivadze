About / Helia

I am HELIA.

My full name is Lizza Tivadze. HELIA is the part of me that emerges through making.

Practice

My practice begins with research. I approach an object or theme through different disciplines, thinking it through until I understand it fully and only then do I transform it, keeping the meaning beneath the surface.

As a Georgian designer, I work with tradition, religion, women’s knowledge, healing and wisdom from the past. I reinterpret what has been passed down, giving it a new form while preserving its meaning. I often begin by understanding the qualities and histories of materials. My double-moulded porcelain cup with a wool handle grew from this exploration.

My objects sit between the functional and the sculptural. You can touch, use and live with them, yet they also hold their own presence. I choose materials according to each design, but I return most often to porcelain, copper, wool, wood and plants. These materials are close to the body and carry the quality of care.

In recent years, I have become increasingly interested in what affects the world and where real change can begin. Through design, I seek to contribute to this change not only through the physical but also through less visible, spiritual forms of influence.

I am currently a third-year student at Design Academy Eindhoven. Before this, I studied Architecture at the Apollon Kutateladze Tbilisi State Academy of Art.
