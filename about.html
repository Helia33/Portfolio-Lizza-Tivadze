About / Helia

I am HELIA.

My full name is Lizza Tivadze. HELIA is the part of me that emerges through making.

Practice

My practice begins with research. I approach an object or a theme through different disciplines and think it through until I understand it, then transform it, keeping the meaning beneath the surface. The making itself is more intuitive: I start from the history of a material and let it lead.

As a Georgian designer, I work with tradition, religion, women’s knowledge and healing. I reinterpret what has been passed down, like tklapi, the sun-dried fruit leather I turned into a lamp, giving it a new form while staying true to where it came from. I return most often to porcelain, copper, wool, wood and plants, materials that are close to the body and carry a quality of care.

My objects sit between the functional and the sculptural. You can touch, use and live with them, yet they also hold their own presence.

What concerns me most is how easily we lose our connection to what is spiritual and inherited. Through design, I want to keep it visible and leave traces for those who come after.

I am currently a third-year student at Design Academy Eindhoven. Before this, I studied Architecture at the Apollon Kutateladze Tbilisi State Academy of Art.
