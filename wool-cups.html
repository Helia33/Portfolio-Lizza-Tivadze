Wool Cups

Porcelain and raw wool: the solid and the soft, held together in one functional object.

Medium
Slip-cast porcelain, raw wool
Process
Double moulding, hand weaving
Year
Spring 2026

I made porcelain cups with hand-woven wool handles. The hard ceramic and soft wool come together in a cup made to be held and used.

Forms of containment

Hand-built forms, wool anchors and vessel studies that led toward the final cup.

From heavy to fluid

I started by pinching clay into simple cup shapes and testing how they held volume. I also explored ways to attach a soft wool handle. Each model helped me move from a heavy, grounded shape towards a lighter double-moulded cup.

Preparation

Felting wool fibres into stripes and arranging the strands before assembly.

Threading

Working the wool through the porcelain cup’s anchor points.

Finishing

Securing, turning and refining the wool handle on the cup.
